A missed detection is a gap in coverage. A false detection is a compliance flag raised against nobody — and a report full of phantom workers gets ignored.

The honest footnote: the rejected model's person pass is itself incomplete (1,225 images with PPE boxes but no person box), so some of that 0.31 may be the dataset being wrong. Both readings support the same decision.
